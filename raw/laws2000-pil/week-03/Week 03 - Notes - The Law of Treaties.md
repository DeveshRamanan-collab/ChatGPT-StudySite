- International Law THE LAW OF TREATIES United Nations, Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (VCLT) (adopted 23 May 1969; entered into force 27 January 1980) • The "treaty on treaties” • Establishes rules, procedures, and guidelines for how treaties are created, drafted, amended, interpreted, and operated. • Only applies to treaties concluded between states (Art. 1 VCLT). • “the law of treaties” refers to the body of legal rules and principles of international law applicable to the making, operation, and termination of a treaty.

### Slide page 32

- International Law THE LAW OF TREATIES Question: what would be the legal effect/consequences of an invalid reservation to a treaty? • A reservation that does not meet the conditions of validity and permissibility is null and void, and therefore devoid of any legal effect. Other states are not obliged to object or accept. ― Solution 1: Reserving State does not become party to the treaty ― Solution 2: Reserving State becomes party to the treaty without the reservation ― Solution 3: Reserving becomes party to the treaty without the reservation, unless “it has expressed a contrary intention”

### Slide page 33

- International Law THE LAW OF TREATIES TREATY INTERPRETATION • Parties have competence to interpret a treaty, but subject to the operation of legal rules • Various ‘rules’ for interpreting treaties have been put forward over the years (plain meaning rule, overall context rule, teleological rule, supplementary rule etc.) • Present regime: Arts 31 & 32, VCLT (reflect customary law)

### Slide page 34

- International Law THE LAW OF TREATIES • Article 31 (1) ‘General rule of interpretation’ A treaty shall be interpreted in good faith in accordance with the ordinary meaning to be given to the terms of the treaty in their context and in the light of its object and purpose. • Article 32 ‘Supplementary means of interpretation’ Recourse may be had to supplementary means of interpretation, including the preparatory work of the treaty and the circumstances of its conclusion…when the interpretation according to article 31: a) leaves the meaning ambiguous or obscure; or b) leads to a result which is manifestly absurd or unreasonable.
