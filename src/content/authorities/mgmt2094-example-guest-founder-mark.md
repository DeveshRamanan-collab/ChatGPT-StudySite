text: Mark has run his own furniture design and manufacturing business for more than 30 years. Wanting to make tangible things and to be environmentally friendly, he started with recycled timber, very little money and a fair bit of knowledge — from an earlier menswear business and about three years as PA to the owner of a large recycled-timber furniture business — working alone from a double garage with an old truck.
