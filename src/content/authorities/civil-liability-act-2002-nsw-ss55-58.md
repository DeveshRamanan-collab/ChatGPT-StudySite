text: |
  56 ... a good samaritan is a person who, in good faith and without expectation of payment or other reward,
  comes to the assistance of a person who is apparently injured or at risk of being injured. 57(1) A good
  samaritan does not incur any personal civil liability in respect of any act or omission done or made by
  the good samaritan in an emergency when assisting a person who is apparently injured or at risk of being
  injured. 58(1) The protection from personal liability conferred by this Part does not apply if it is the
  good samaritan's intentional or negligent act or omission that caused the injury or risk of injury ... (2)
  [nor where] the ability of the good samaritan to exercise reasonable care and skill was significantly
  impaired by reason of the good samaritan being under the influence of alcohol or a drug voluntarily
  consumed ... and the good samaritan failed to exercise reasonable care and skill.
gloss: Protects a person who voluntarily and in good faith assists someone apparently injured or at risk from personal civil liability for their emergency response, unless they caused the risk themselves or were relevantly impaired.
