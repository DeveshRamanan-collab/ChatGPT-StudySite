title: ICJ Statute art 38(1)

text: |
  The article directs the Court to apply, in deciding disputes submitted to it: international conventions
  (treaties); international custom; "the general principles of law recognized by civilized nations"; and, as
  a subsidiary means for the determination of rules of law, judicial decisions and "the teachings of the most
  highly qualified publicists of the various nations".
