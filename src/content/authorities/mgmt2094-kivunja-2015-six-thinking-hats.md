text: Critical thinking and problem solving are essential for success in the 21st century economy, yet they are rarely taught explicitly. Kivunja sets out de Bono's six thinking hats — black for caution, blue for organising thinking (thinking about thinking), green for creativity, red for emotions, white for information and yellow for optimism — as a simple metaphor that lets a thinker take one perspective, take it off and switch to another. The hats answer de Bono's three difficulties in practical thinking (emotions, helplessness and confusion); there is no set sequence for wearing them, and several are often used at once.
