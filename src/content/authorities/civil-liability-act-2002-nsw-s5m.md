title: Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW) s 5M
kind: statute
text: |
  (1) A person (the defendant) does not owe a duty of care to another person who engages in a recreational
  activity (the plaintiff) to take care in respect of a risk of the activity if the risk was the subject of a
  risk warning to the plaintiff. (3) ... a risk warning ... is a warning that is given in a manner that is
  reasonably likely to result in people being warned of the risk before engaging in the recreational
  activity. The defendant is not required to establish that the person received or understood the warning or
  was capable of receiving or understanding the warning. (4) A risk warning can be given orally or in
  writing (including by means of a sign or otherwise). (5) A risk warning need not be specific to the
  particular risk and can be a general warning of risks that include the particular risk concerned.
gloss: Removes the duty of care for a recreational risk once a suitably general risk warning has been given, whether or not the plaintiff actually understood it.
