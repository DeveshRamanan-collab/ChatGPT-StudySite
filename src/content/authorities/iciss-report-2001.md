text: |-
  The ICISS was initiated by Canada in 2000, chaired by Gareth Evans (former Australian Minister of Foreign Affairs)
  and Mohamed Sahnoun, and delivered its final report in December 2001. Its focus was a shift in language from a right
  to intervene to a responsibility to protect the victims: state sovereignty implies responsibility; when a state is
  "unwilling or unable" to halt or avert serious harm to its population there is an international responsibility to
  prevent, to react (military force as a last resort) and to rebuild.
gloss: The report that coined the responsibility to protect, reframing humanitarian intervention as a responsibility rather than a right.
