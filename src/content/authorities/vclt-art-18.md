title: Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties 1969 art 18
kind: treaty
text: |
  A State is obliged to refrain from acts which would defeat the object and purpose of a treaty when it has
  signed the treaty (or exchanged instruments constituting it) subject to ratification, acceptance or
  approval, until it has made clear its intention not to become a party; or when it has expressed its consent
  to be bound, pending entry into force, provided entry into force is not unduly delayed.
gloss: A signatory not yet bound must still not defeat the treaty's object and purpose.
