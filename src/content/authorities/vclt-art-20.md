title: Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties 1969 art 20
kind: treaty
text: |
  A reservation expressly authorised by a treaty does not require any acceptance; other parties may accept or
  object to a permissible reservation (acceptance makes both parties to the treaty and the reservation;
  objection means the treaty does not enter into force between the reserving and objecting states); a
  reservation becomes effective once at least one other contracting state has accepted it; a reservation to
  the constituent instrument of an international organisation requires acceptance by that organisation's
  competent organ.
gloss: Sets what happens once a reservation is permissible — acceptance, objection, and the one-acceptance rule.
