text: A paper-towel brand sells rolls at 50 cents with a marginal cost of 15 cents, so 1,000 rolls earn $350 profit. A 20%-off promotion sold 1,200 rolls for $300 profit; a 40%-off promotion sold 1,800 rolls (an 80% increase) but earned only $270. To keep the usual $350 profit at 40% off, the firm would have to sell more than 2,300 units, an increase of 133%.
gloss: Shows that the profit levers of cost, volume and price are related nonlinearly, so managers overvalue volume and undervalue getting the price right.
