text: |
  Art 49(1): "An injured State may only take countermeasures against a State which is responsible for an
  internationally wrongful act in order to induce that State to comply with its obligations…". Countermeasures
  must not breach jus cogens obligations (arts 50, 53), must be proportional (art 51), and must meet procedural
  requirements — negotiate, notify, give a chance to cease, terminate once the wrongful act has ceased (arts 52,
  53) (as summarised on the Week 7 slides).
