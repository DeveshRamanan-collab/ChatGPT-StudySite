  The UN General Assembly's 2005 World Summit Outcome endorsed the responsibility to protect (the lecturer cited
  paragraphs 138 and 139). The doctrine as endorsed rests on three pillars: (1) governments have the primary duty to
  their populations — whether citizens or not — to protect them from genocide, war crimes, ethnic cleansing and crimes
  against humanity and their incitement; (2) the international community commits to assist states in meeting these
  obligations; (3) a responsibility to respond through the UN mechanisms. It only asks the permanent members to
  "refrain" from exercising the veto in cases of the four international crimes.
