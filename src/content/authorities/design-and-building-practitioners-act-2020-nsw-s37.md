title: Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 (NSW) s 37
kind: statute
text: (1) A person who carries out construction work has a duty to exercise reasonable care to avoid economic loss caused by defects— (a) in or related to a building for which the work is done, and (b) arising from the construction work. (2) The duty of care is owed to each owner of the land in relation to which the construction work is carried out and to each subsequent owner of the land. (3) A person to whom the duty of care is owed is entitled to damages for the breach of the duty as if the duty were a duty established by the common law.
