title: Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW) s 5H
kind: statute
text: |
  (1) A person (the defendant) does not owe a duty of care to another person (the plaintiff) to warn of an
  obvious risk to the plaintiff. (2) This section does not apply if— (a) the plaintiff has requested advice
  or information about the risk from the defendant, or (b) the defendant is required by a written law to
  warn the plaintiff of the risk, or (c) the defendant is a professional and the risk is a risk of the death
  of or personal injury to the plaintiff from the provision of a professional service by the defendant. (3)
  Subsection (2) does not give rise to a presumption of a duty to warn of a risk in the circumstances
  referred to in that subsection.
