title: Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW) s 30
kind: statute
text: |
  (1) This section applies to the liability of a person (the defendant) for pure mental harm to a person
  (the plaintiff) arising wholly or partly from mental or nervous shock in connection with another person
  (the victim) being killed, injured or put in peril by the act or omission of the defendant. (2) The
  plaintiff is not entitled to recover damages for pure mental harm unless— (a) the plaintiff witnessed, at
  the scene, the victim being killed, injured or put in peril, or (b) the plaintiff is a close member of the
  family of the victim.
gloss: Limits recovery for pure mental harm arising from shock to scene-witnesses and close family members of the victim.
