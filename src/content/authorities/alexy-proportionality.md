title: Robert Alexy on proportionality (rules and principles; the weight formula)
kind: principle
text: "Alexy distinguishes rules (definitive commands) from principles (optimisation requirements). Principles must be realised to the greatest extent possible, given legal and factual constraints. Proportionality is necessarily connected to the adjudication of constitutional rights because rights are principles. Three-part test: suitability (the measure must be capable of achieving the legitimate aim); necessity (there must be no less restrictive means to achieve the same goal); proportionality in the narrow sense, or balancing (the benefits of the measure must outweigh the detriment to the right infringed). The \"weight formula\" formalises balancing: it considers the intensity of interference, the importance of the competing principle, and the degree of certainty about these factors."
gloss: Rights as principles to be optimised, so limits on them are tested for suitability, necessity and balance.
