title: Articles on Responsibility of States for Internationally Wrongful Acts (2001) art 24
kind: article
text: |
  "1. The wrongfulness of an act of a State not in conformity with an international obligation of that State is
  precluded if the author of the act in question has no other reasonable way, in a situation of distress, of
  saving the author's life or the lives of other persons entrusted to the author's care. 2. Paragraph 1 does not
  apply if: the situation of distress is due, either alone or in combination with other factors, to the conduct
  of the State invoking it; or the act in question is likely to create a comparable or greater peril."
gloss: Distress excuses an act that was the only reasonable way to save lives, unless self-induced or more dangerous.
