  Article 3: Every internationally wrongful act of an international organization entails the international
  responsibility of that organization. Article 4: There is an internationally wrongful act of an international
  organization when conduct consisting of an action or omission: (a) is attributable to that organization under
  international law; and (b) constitutes a breach of an international obligation of that organization.
