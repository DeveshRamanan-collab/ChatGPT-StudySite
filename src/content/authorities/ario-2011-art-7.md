text: |
  The conduct of an organ of a State or an organ or agent of an international organization that is placed at the
  disposal of another international organization shall be considered under international law an act of the
  latter organization if the organization exercises effective control over that conduct.
gloss: Effective control decides whether seconded conduct (e.g. peacekeepers) is attributed to the receiving IO or stays with the contributing state.
