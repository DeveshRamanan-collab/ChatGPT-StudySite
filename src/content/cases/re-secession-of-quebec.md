  Quebec had held referendums on separating from Canada. The question of whether Quebec could unilaterally secede

  Whether Quebec had the legal right to unilaterally secede from Canada under Canadian law and under international

  Unilateral secession is not consistent with international law or the Canadian Constitution. Distinguishing
