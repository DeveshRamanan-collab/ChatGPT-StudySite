facts: The plaintiff's leg, injured by the defendant's negligence, was prone to give way. He descended a steep staircase without a handrail, unaided; the leg gave way and he suffered a severe ankle fracture.
issue: Whether the defendant was liable for the second injury, or whether the plaintiff's own conduct broke the chain of causation.
held: "The plaintiff's unreasonable conduct was a novus actus interveniens: the chain was broken and the defendant was not liable for the second injury."
ratio: A plaintiff's own unreasonable conduct after the original injury can break the chain of causation, so that the original wrongdoer is not liable for harm after the break.
