citation: (2001) 206 CLR 1
aglc: Tepko Pty Ltd v Water Board (2001) 206 CLR 1
facts: Landowners wanting to subdivide needed a costing for water supply, which their bank wanted. The Water Board refused several times, then reluctantly gave a 'ball-park' figure. The landowners showed it to their bank; the figure was heavily overstated and the refinancing arrangements collapsed.
issue: Whether the Board owed the landowners a duty of care in giving the estimate.
held: "No duty: the Board neither knew nor should have known of the reliance, and reliance on a reluctant, provisional estimate was not reasonable. Dissenting judges would have found a duty, pointing to the statutory authority, its special knowledge and the serious business consequences."
ratio: A duty for negligent misstatement needs known reliance (actual or constructive), an assumption of responsibility, or both; reliance on a reluctant, expressly provisional estimate is not reasonable.
significance: Shows both the knowledge requirement and the reasonableness of reliance failing on the same facts.
why-here: "The authority for reluctant, provisional or \"ball-park\" figures: no known reliance, no reasonable reliance, no duty."
