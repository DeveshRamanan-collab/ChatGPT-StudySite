facts: A process server entered the plaintiff's land for the purpose of serving a summons on him.
issue: Whether the implied licence to approach a dwelling extends to entry for the purpose of serving a summons.
held: The implied licence did not extend to service of a summons; the entry was a trespass.
ratio: The implied licence to enter is confined to lawful communication with the occupier and does not authorise entry for purposes the occupier has not consented to, such as serving legal process.
significance: High Court authority that a person exercising an otherwise lawful function can still trespass by exceeding the implied licence.
why-here: Shows the implied licence is narrow and purpose-specific — exceeding it, even for a lawful function, is trespass.
