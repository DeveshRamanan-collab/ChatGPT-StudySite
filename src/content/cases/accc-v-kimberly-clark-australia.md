issue: Whether the "flushable" and "made in Australia" claims were misleading or deceptive under the Australian Consumer Law.
held: The Federal Court found the "made in Australia" claim was false and misleading — the wipes were made elsewhere — but ruled that Kimberly-Clark had not made false and misleading claims about the "flushable" wipes; that ruling was upheld on appeal by the Full Federal Court. Kimberly-Clark had faced similar actions in the US and decided to settle out of court.
ratio: A claim is assessed under s 18 by asking whether the conduct was misleading or deceptive; no duty of care or negligence need be shown.
significance: An example of statutory liability for misstatements operating without the duty of care framework.
