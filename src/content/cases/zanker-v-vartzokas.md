facts: A girl accepted a lift in the defendant's van; after she declined his request for sexual favours in exchange for money, he told her, while the van was moving, "I am going to take you to my mate's house. He will really fix you up."
issue: Whether a threat of future harm, made while the plaintiff had no means of immediate escape, was sufficiently imminent to be an assault.
held: The threat was sufficiently imminent in the circumstances, even though the harm threatened was not to happen at that instant, because the plaintiff was confined in a moving van with no means of escape until the threat could be carried out.
ratio: "\"Imminent\" does not mean immediate; a threat is imminent if there is no effective means of escape before it can be carried out."
significance: Leading Australian authority that imminence in assault is assessed against the plaintiff's practical ability to escape the threatened contact.
