facts: |
  A case before the Inter-American Court of Human Rights concerning Honduras; the materials cite it only for the
  principle on acts of state agents outside the limits of their authority.
issue: |
  Whether a state is responsible for acts of its agents who contravene internal law or overstep the limits of
  their authority.
held: |
  Responsibility arises "independent of whether the organ or official has contravened provisions of internal law
  or overstepped the limits of his authority: under international law a State is responsible for the acts of its
  agents undertaken in their official capacity and for their omissions, even when those agents act outside the
  sphere of their authority or violate internal law" (para 170).
ratio: |
  Acts of state agents in their official capacity are attributable to the state even when ultra vires or
  contrary to internal law.
significance: |
  Recognises the rule now in art 7 ARSIWA; the rationale is that the state should be able to monitor and control
  its organs and the entities working for it.
