issue: Whether a defendant who did not directly touch the plaintiff, but caused nervous shock through a false statement, could be liable in tort.

ratio: Intentional infliction of physical harm through indirect means (such as words causing nervous shock) is actionable where the act was calculated to cause harm and did cause it.
