aglc: Lincoln Hunt Aust v Willesee (1986) 4 NSWLR 457
facts: Television reporters and a camera crew entered the plaintiff's commercial premises to film, rather than to seek information or conduct business with the occupier as a genuine client.
issue: Whether entry onto commercial premises open to the public is within any implied licence when the entrant's purpose is filming rather than bona fide business.
held: The entry was a trespass.
ratio: The implied licence to enter commercial premises open to the public extends only to bona fide business with, or genuine use as a client or customer of, the occupier; entry for an unrelated purpose, such as filming, exceeds the licence.
significance: Applies the implied-licence concept to commercial rather than residential premises.
why-here: Use for problems about media or filming crews entering business premises — the same "purpose within the licence" logic as the residential cases.
