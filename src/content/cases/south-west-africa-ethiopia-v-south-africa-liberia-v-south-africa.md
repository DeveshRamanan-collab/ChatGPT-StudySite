issue: What must be shown for a dispute to exist between the applicant and the respondent?
held: |-
  A dispute exists when it is demonstrated that the two sides "hold clearly opposite views" with respect to the issue
  in question — when "the claim of one party is positively opposed by the other" and "the respondent was aware, or
  could not have been unaware, that its views were 'positively opposed' by the applicant".
ratio: "A dispute requires clearly opposite views: positive opposition of one party's claim by the other, of which the respondent was or could not have been unaware."
significance: The core objective test for the existence of a dispute; the lecture adds that it requires more than mere friction and some communication between the parties, though the threshold is low.
