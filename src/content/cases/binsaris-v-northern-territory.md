facts: Tear gas was deployed and touched the eyes of the plaintiff.
issue: Whether the physical-interference element of battery requires the defendant's body to touch the plaintiff directly.
held: Contact through an instrument such as tear gas is sufficient; the defendant's body need not physically touch the plaintiff.
ratio: Battery's physical-contact element is satisfied by contact through an object, substance or weapon, not only by direct bodily touching.
significance: Confirms battery covers indirect physical contact (eg gas, a weapon or a car) as long as it is directly caused.
