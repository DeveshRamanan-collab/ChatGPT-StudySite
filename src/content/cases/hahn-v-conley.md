facts: A grandfather was looking after a 3-year-old girl. She called out from across the road; he answered the call; she crossed the road and was struck by a car. The defendant driver claimed an indemnity from the grandfather, assessed at 25% of the total damages.
issue: Whether the girl had a cause of action against her grandfather.
held: Parents may become liable to the child if the child is led into danger by their actions; but on the facts the grandfather, in loco parentis, owed the girl no duty of care to protect her against the danger — he did not call the girl to come to him.
ratio: A parent (or person in loco parentis) can owe a child a duty not to lead the child into danger, but has no general duty to protect the child from every danger.
significance: Marks the limits of a parent's or carer's duty to the child.
