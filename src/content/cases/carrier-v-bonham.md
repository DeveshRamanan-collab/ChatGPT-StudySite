issue: Whether the defendant's mental illness affected the standard of care expected of him.
held: The Queensland Court of Appeal held that the defendant's mental condition had no effect on the standard of care expected.
ratio: "McPherson JA: \"it would be impossible to devise a standard by which the tortious liability of such persons could be judged as a class\". There is no such thing as a 'normal' condition of unsound mind in those who suffer that affliction.\""
significance: Mental illness or disability does not lower the reasonable person standard, unlike age (McHale v Watson).
