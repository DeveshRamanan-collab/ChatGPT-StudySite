facts: CHEP owned timber pallets that were used by Bunnings; Bunnings used the pallets both to store or display goods delivered on them, and to transport its own goods.
issue: Whether Bunnings's use of CHEP's pallets amounted to a sufficiently serious interference to be conversion.
held: Using the pallets to store or display the goods delivered on them was not conversion, but using empty pallets to transport Bunnings's own goods was conversion.
ratio: Whether a dealing with goods is a "sufficiently serious interference" amounting to conversion depends on the nature of the use, not simply on possession or handling; courts can differ on where the line falls.
significance: Illustrates the fine, fact-sensitive judgment required to decide whether a dealing with goods is serious enough to be conversion.
