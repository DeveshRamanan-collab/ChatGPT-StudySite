issue: On what basis a doctor may lawfully treat a patient who is unable to consent, in the patient's best interests.

ratio: |-
  Necessity justifies medical treatment of a person unable to consent where it is not practicable to
  communicate with them, and the treatment given is what a reasonable person would give, acting in the
  assisted person's best interests; for a permanently incapacitated patient the doctor need not wait to
  obtain consent, but for a temporarily incapacitated patient the doctor should do no more than necessary
  until the patient recovers.
