issue: When a person giving information or advice owes the recipient a duty of reasonable care.
held: Barwick CJ stated that where information or advice is given on a serious matter, the speaker realises (or should realise) they are being trusted, and it is reasonable for the recipient to act on it, a duty of reasonable care follows. The materials do not state the final outcome of the appeal.
ratio: A duty of care in giving information or advice arises where the matter is serious, the speaker realises or should realise they are trusted, and it is reasonable for the recipient to rely on it.
