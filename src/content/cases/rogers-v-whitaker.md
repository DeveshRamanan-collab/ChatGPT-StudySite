facts: An eye surgeon failed to give a warning about a complication that arose in one in 14,000 cases.
issue: What standard of care applies to a professional — and whether it is set by professional practice (the Bolam principle).
held: The High Court held that the standard of care "is that of ordinary skilled person exercising and professing to have that special skill", and rejected the Bolam principle (that the standard of care is determined in line with medical practice).
ratio: A professional is judged against the ordinary skilled person exercising and professing to have that special skill, not simply against what the profession does.
significance: The common law professional standard. Section 5O of the Civil Liability Act now incorporates a modified version of the Bolam principle that Rogers rejected.
