  Yunis, a Lebanese national, was involved in the hijacking of Royal Jordanian flight 402 in 1985 with American
  citizens aboard the aircraft. He was captured in international waters by the FBI and brought to the US to stand
  trial.
issue: |
  Whether the US had jurisdiction over a non-US citizen for crimes (hostage taking) committed outside the United
  States.
held: |
  The US argued it had jurisdiction because Yunis's actions were against American citizens and so violated US
  law; the court found that the US had jurisdiction.
ratio: |
  A state may assert jurisdiction over an offence committed abroad on the ground that its nationals were the
  victims (passive personality).
significance: |
  The leading example of the passive personality principle — which remains controversial and is better treated
