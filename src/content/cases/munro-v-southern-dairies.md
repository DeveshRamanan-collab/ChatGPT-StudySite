issue: What standard is used to assess whether interference with the plaintiff's comfort is substantial and unreasonable.
held: Considering all the factors, the keeping of horses caused a nuisance to the plaintiff.
ratio: The standard of comfort for private nuisance is assessed objectively, by reference to the standards of an ordinary person in the claimant's position, not a person of "abnormal sensitiveness".
significance: Establishes the objective "ordinary person" test for the substantial-and-unreasonable element of private nuisance.
