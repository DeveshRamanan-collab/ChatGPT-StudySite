facts: A passenger who missed his ferry was not allowed to leave the wharf without paying a further penny.
issue: Whether requiring payment of a further fee before a person already on the premises could leave amounted to false imprisonment.
held: No false imprisonment was made out, because the passenger had entered the wharf on a conditional term (pay to leave), which gave the ferry company a lawful excuse for the restraint.
ratio: A restraint imposed under a reasonable condition the plaintiff accepted on entry, which the plaintiff can satisfy to be released, is not false imprisonment.
significance: Shows that a lawful condition attached to entering premises can defeat a false imprisonment claim.
