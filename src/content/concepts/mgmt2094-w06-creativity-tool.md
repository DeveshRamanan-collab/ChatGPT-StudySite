In the tutorial you are the new product development team for the Boring, Drab and Awful Floor Company. Your job
is to develop 100 new floor covering product ideas in 10 minutes. Avoid just adding an adjective like "soft" or "blue".
Use the creativity tool. Have fun and create energy!

The creativity tool has four columns of prompts:

- **Try to** — slide it, dissolve it, compress it, roll it, stack it, inflate it, divide it, clamp it…
- **Make it** — transparent, movable, sticky, flexible, hot, cold, slippery, pop up, flicker, foamy…
- **Add or subtract** — springs, elastic, traction, nets, filters, waves, vacuum, rubber, gears, energy, power, time…
- **Imagine that you** — can read minds, are the problem, have a magic wand, have unlimited money, can travel in time,
  can make yourself smaller…

The debrief asks: How many ideas do you have? What helped you be more creative? Pick your top 3 innovative ideas, and
report one idea to the rest of the class.

The rule against just adding an adjective pushes each idea to change the product itself, and the tool supplies the
push: each prompt is applied to the floor covering in turn.

Exam use: when asked how a team could generate many ideas, apply prompts from each column to the product, then
converge on the top three.
