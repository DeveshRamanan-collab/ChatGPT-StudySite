The Week 6 tutorial is there to think about and practise convergent and divergent thinking for creativity and
innovation. Its slide "Patterns of Creative Thinking" sets out the two.

**Diverging**: defer judgement; look for lots of ideas; accept all ideas; make yourself "stretch"; take time to
"simmer"; seek combination — bring together odd combinations.

**Converging**: look for what is really new; what gets people excited, even if not very realistic; avoid premature
closure; take the risk to look at difficult issues or "sneaky spots"; develop a sense of "affirmative judgement"; keep
your eyes on your objective.

Converging, on this slide, is not simply rejecting ideas. It still values what excites people over what is realistic,
still avoids closing too early, and judges affirmatively, while keeping the objective in view.

In the Floor Company Exercise, generating 100 floor covering ideas in 10 minutes is diverging; picking your top 3
innovative ideas and reporting one to the class is converging.

Exam use: classify a team's behaviour by pattern. Judging ideas while still generating them breaks "defer judgement";
settling on the first workable idea is premature closure.
