The lecture closes with questions to ask myself:

- What have I learnt from the process?
- What do I now know, that I didn't before?
- What insights have I gained?
- What were the challenges that I encountered/observed, and how well did I and the team handle them?
- What would I do better or differently next time?
- What have I learnt from this?

Then the warning: "Remember ultimately do not just describe; you must analyse and reflect!" Review the criteria within
the group project, and keep the marking rubric in mind.

Description alone is not enough. The slides tie the analysis to your group project and experiences "with theoretical
underpinnings", and the six questions turn the experience into learning: what I now know, what insights I gained, and
what I would do better or differently next time. In the recording the lecturer says the reflection is about your
experience of working in a team — what happened, the dynamics you experienced, what you did and what you achieved — and
that you are not just describing things: you need to analyse them.

Exam use: when judging a reflective passage, ask whether each paragraph answers one of the six questions; if it only
narrates events, it is description.
