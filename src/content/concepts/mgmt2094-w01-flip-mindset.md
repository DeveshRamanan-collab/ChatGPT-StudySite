What is the unit about? Creating a different mind set: **zig when everyone else is zagging**. The lecturer
keeps returning to one question: what does that mean to you?

The terminology now used is **flip**: repeatedly moving between different perspectives and paradigms, now done in
"real" time and on demand. It is a **flexible mindset** that stimulates a way of thinking and an approach to life, and
a more **counter-intuitive approach** that reflects the way business is heading today: to be more creative and
innovative.

Why now? The lecture gives two reasons. The world of business is fast moving and changing very quickly, so you need
to move between perspectives with ease. And with the advance of AI, technical knowledge is not enough, because AI can
provide any organisation or entrepreneur with technical knowledge and skill; creativity and innovation, including
working with AI to boost them, are what set you apart.

Example: one of Bob Iger's first moves as Disney's CEO in 2005 was to put ABC's *Lost* and *Desperate Housewives* on
iTunes, the first deal of its kind and a bet "seemingly at odds with the company's business model" (Lev-Ram 2015). Tim
Cook: "He understands the tradition of Disney but isn't wedded to it."

Exam use: name the flip as the mindset the unit builds, then show it in a counter-intuitive business decision.
