What is an innovative leader? The slides list five traits: passionate about the company; obsessed with restoring innovation; willing to take risks; can manage risks; can start projects quickly.

Each characteristic of an innovation culture has a matching characteristic in its leader:

| Innovation culture | Innovation leader |
| --- | --- |
| Organisation-wide commitment to the business and to innovation | Passionate about innovation |
| Experiments proactively | Willing to experiment |
| Manages risk | Realistic and informed |
| Can implement ideas quickly | Capable of rapid project execution |
| Produces and cultivates innovators | Able to attract and nurture innovators |

Risk appears twice: the leader is willing to take risks and also able to manage them, which is why "realistic and informed" sits opposite "manages risk".

"How to lead innovation" opens with Steve Jobs: "Innovation distinguishes between a leader and a follower."

Example: Sam Nickless began at Gilbert + Tobin with a strategic review of the legal industry, then tested LegalVision with mystery shoppers and a review by the firm's top lawyers before buying a stake: realistic and informed. Shelley Simpson tried everything early on, yet stays wary of outside investors and of expanding too quickly.

Exam use: match each leader trait to its culture trait and give one behaviour as evidence.
