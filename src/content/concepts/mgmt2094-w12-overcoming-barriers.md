How to overcome the barriers: learning to manage innovation.

1. **Shared vision, leadership and the will to innovate**: a clearly articulated and shared sense of purpose; stretching strategic intent; top management commitment.
2. **Appropriate culture**: encouraging creativity; enabling learning and interaction; balancing between "organic and mechanistic".
3. **Key individuals**: promoters, champions and other roles which facilitate innovation.
4. **Effective team working**: teams at cross-functional and inter-organisational level; investment in team selection and building.
5. **Continuing and stretching individual development**: education and training of employees to ensure a high level of skills and competence.
6. **Extensive communication**: within and between the organisation and outside.
7. **Creative climate**: a positive approach to creative ideas, supported by relevant motivation systems.
8. **Learning organisation**: high involvement within and outside the firm in proactive experimentation; knowledge capture and dissemination.

**Conclusion.** Enterprises should emphasise planning and controlling systems with a high degree of flexibility; respect for individual initiative and personal growth; and tolerance for mistakes, allowing room for failure.

Example: Shelley Simpson's team is expected to "lean in" and is rewarded for it (creative climate). Gilbert + Tobin's stake in LegalVision gives it the ability to learn from disruption as it reaches its market (learning organisation).
