Art 33(1) UN Charter includes "resort to regional agencies or arrangements". Art 52 permits regional arrangements
consistent with the UN's Purposes and Principles; members "shall make every effort to achieve pacific settlement of
local disputes" through them "before referring them to the Security Council", and the Security Council shall
encourage this (art 52(1)–(3)).

- **African Union** — Peace and Security Council Protocol, art 4: peaceful settlement of disputes and conflicts;
  early responses to contain crises before they become full-blown conflicts.
- **OAS (1948)** — arts 24–25: disputes between members go to listed peaceful procedures — direct negotiation, good
  offices, mediation, investigation and conciliation, judicial settlement, arbitration, and any the parties agree.
- **Arab League** — art 5: no recourse to force between members; the Council mediates disputes that may lead to war.
  Art 7: unanimous decisions bind all; majority decisions bind only those that accept them.
- **Europe** — the Council of Europe's European Convention for the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes; the OSCE's Court
  of Conciliation and Arbitration in Geneva (1992); NATO.
- **ASEAN** — Treaty of Amity and Cooperation, art 2(d): settlement by peaceful means; Chapter IV (arts 13–17).

**WTO.** Fixed upper limits on tariffs (art II and schedules), a general prohibition on non-tariff barriers (art
XI), and non-discrimination (most-favoured-nation and national treatment). Disputes go through Annex 2 of the WTO
Agreement, the Understanding on Rules and Procedures Governing the Settlement of Disputes.

Exam use: for a local or trade dispute, name the regional or WTO route (art 52).
