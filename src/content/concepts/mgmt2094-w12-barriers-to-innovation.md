The slides sort barriers to innovation into two groups.

**External barriers**:
- market-related barriers;
- government and its policies;
- others: technical, societal and inter-organisational barriers.

**Internal barriers**:
- people-related barriers;
- structural barriers;
- strategy-related barriers.

The first question is where the obstacle sits: outside the organisation (a market, a government, a technology, society, another organisation) or inside it (its people, its structure, its strategy). Then name the type.

Keep barriers apart from the seven challenges of managing innovation: the slides list them separately, and follow the barriers with how to overcome them (learning to manage innovation).

Example: Shelley Simpson calls compliance one of her biggest hurdles: knowing the duties and taxes in each market she sells into, a barrier from government and its policies. Gilbert + Tobin decided against launching its own low-cost firm because getting the incentives and culture right beside a traditional legal model would be very hard, an obstacle inside the firm.

Exam use: classify the barrier as external or internal, name its type, then pair it with a component for overcoming it.
