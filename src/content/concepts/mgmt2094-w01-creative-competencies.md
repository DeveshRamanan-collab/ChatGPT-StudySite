**Individual competencies**

- **Exploring problems**: some people are naturally curious; others can develop it by exploring problems
  systematically.
- **Building up willpower**: without it we cannot keep looking for solutions to the problems we find.
- **Self-awareness**: understanding our strengths and weaknesses, to leverage the strengths and minimise the weaknesses.
- **Being open to failure and feedback from others**: learn from failure, see it in a positive light, and use
  feedback, especially when you fail.

**Team/group competencies**

- **Supporting ideas from team members**: consistently championing other people's ideas.
- **Practising psychological safety**: an environment where everybody feels safe, not to relax but to take risks;
  otherwise people hold back their ideas for fear of being seen as stupid or incompetent.
- **Creating and sharing visions**: relying on each other's different expertise.
- **Pushing the frontiers**: a team that feels supported is empowered to push the boundaries, the fuel for innovation.

Example: Iger gave each Disney business its own CTO, saying it gives them "the ability to experiment and try even if
they fail … without a corporate watchdog looking over their shoulder all the time" (Lev-Ram 2015).

Exam use: sort the behaviour into individual or team, then name the competency.
