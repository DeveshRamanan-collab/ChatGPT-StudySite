plain: Only the parts of a Security Council resolution that decide something bind states; parts that merely recommend do not. A binding decision also overrides a conflicting treaty obligation.
concrete: "The lecturer warned against treating a resolution as binding as a whole. One resolution can contain paragraphs that are only recommendations and paragraphs that are decisions — often both — and only the decisions bind. Which is which usually turns on the words the Council uses, chiefly its verbs, and some wording is debated. Art 39 draws the same line: the Council \"shall make recommendations, or decide\"."
formal: "Art 25: members \"agree to accept and carry out the decisions of the Security Council in accordance with the present Charter\". Recommendations — e.g. \"appropriate procedures or methods of adjustment\" (art 36(1)), or recommendations under art 39 — do not bind. Under art 103 Charter obligations prevail over obligations under \"any other international agreement\". The General Assembly only recommends (arts 11(2), 14)."
terms:
  - term: decision
    gloss: A Council act members have agreed to accept and carry out (art 25); binding on all members.
  - term: recommendation
    gloss: Advice from the Council (or the Assembly) that does not bind, e.g. procedures recommended under art 36(1).
misconceptions:
  - claim: Every paragraph of a Security Council resolution binds member states.
    tempting: A resolution is adopted as a single instrument.
    correction: The lecture stressed only decisions bind under art 25; recommendations in the same resolution do not. The wording, usually the verbs, shows which is which.
  - claim: A state is bound only if it voted for the resolution.
    tempting: Consent usually governs international obligations.
    correction: By art 25 members have agreed in advance to carry out Council decisions; a decision binds every member, however it voted and whether or not it sits on the Council.
  - claim: A Chapter VI recommendation binds the parties like a Chapter VII decision.
    tempting: Both come from the Council, whose decisions bind.
    correction: Under Chapter VI the Council recommends (art 36(1)); recommendations do not bind. Binding force attaches to decisions.
