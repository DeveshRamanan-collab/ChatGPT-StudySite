terms:
  - term: declaratory theory
    gloss: Recognition only acknowledges statehood that already exists once the criteria are met; it is symbolic. The most widely held view today.
  - term: constitutive theory
    gloss: "Recognition by other states creates the state and confers legal personality: no recognition, no existence. Still has some weight."
  - term: collective recognition
    gloss: Recognition by states acting together in an international organisation. UN admission needs a Security Council recommendation (vetoable) and a two-thirds General Assembly vote.
  - term: non-recognition
    gloss: Refusing to recognise an entity whose criteria were achieved by unlawful force or egregious violations, especially of jus cogens.
    example: The TRNC, recognised only by Turkey.
