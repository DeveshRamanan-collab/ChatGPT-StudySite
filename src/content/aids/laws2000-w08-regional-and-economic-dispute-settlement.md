plain: Regional organisations and the WTO offer their own peaceful ways to settle disputes. The UN Charter asks states to try regional routes for local disputes before going to the Security Council.
concrete: The Arab League, founded in 1945 and headquartered in Cairo, now has 22 members. Its Charter forbids recourse to force to settle disputes between members (art 5). The League's Council mediates disputes that may lead to war, deciding on arbitration and mediation by majority vote. But under art 7, unanimous Council decisions bind all members, while majority decisions bind only the members that accept them.
formal: "Art 33(1) UN Charter includes \"resort to regional agencies or arrangements\". Art 52: regional arrangements consistent with the UN's Purposes and Principles are permitted; members \"shall make every effort to achieve pacific settlement of local disputes\" through them \"before referring them to the Security Council\", which shall encourage this. WTO disputes go through Annex 2 of the WTO Agreement, the Understanding on Rules and Procedures Governing the Settlement of Disputes."
