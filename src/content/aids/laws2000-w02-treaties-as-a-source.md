formal: "A treaty is \"an international agreement concluded between States in written form and governed by international law\" (VCLT art 2(1)(a)).     correction: "The name does not matter: agreements, protocols, covenants and exchanges of letters can all be treaties. What counts is whether the parties intended binding obligations governed by international law."
  - claim: A widely ratified treaty binds every state.
    tempting: A treaty with most of the world as parties looks like general law.
    correction: A treaty binds its parties, because becoming a party implies consent. A non-party is bound only if the same rule exists in another source, such as custom.

    tempting: Treaties are made between governments, not parliaments.
    correction: A state party must ensure its domestic law and practice are consistent with the treaty and should not legislate contrary to it (slide 5; pacta sunt servanda).
