terms:
  - term: Established category
    gloss: A relationship the courts have already recognised as giving rise to a duty of care, so duty is generally uncontroversial.
  - term: Paradigm case
    gloss: An act causing physical injury or property damage, with nothing special about the parties; generally only reasonable foreseeability is required.
  - term: Novel case
    gloss: A relationship outside the established categories; more than reasonable foreseeability is needed.
