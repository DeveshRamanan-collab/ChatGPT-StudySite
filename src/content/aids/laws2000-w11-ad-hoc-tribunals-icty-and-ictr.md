terms:
  - term: ad hoc tribunal
    gloss: A temporary international criminal tribunal created for one conflict — here by the Security Council under Chapter VII, not by treaty.
  - term: Residual Mechanism (MICT)

    correction: Art 1 of its Statute limits it to serious violations of IHL committed in the territory of the former Yugoslavia since 1991.
