plain: UN peacekeeping missions are run by the UN itself to keep a peace, not enforce one. Traditionally they need the parties' consent, stay impartial and use force only in self-defence — though that is changing.
concrete: In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, the UN mission (MONUSCO) has been authorised to use force to carry out its mandate, including targeted offensive operations — far from the traditional rule of force only in self-defence. Traditional principles: consent of the parties; impartiality; non-use of force except in self-defence. Practice is evolving: transitional administration; missions authorised to use force (DRC, Mali)."
