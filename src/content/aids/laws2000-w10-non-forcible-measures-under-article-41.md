terms:
  - term: comprehensive sanctions
    gloss: Financial, trade and other economic measures against a whole state — e.g. Iraq in the 1990s, North Korea today; controversial for their impact on populations.
  - term: targeted ("smart") sanctions
    gloss: Measures aimed at specific commodities (e.g. conflict diamonds) or at particular groups, corporations or individuals (travel bans, asset freezes).
  - term: sanctions committee
    gloss: A Council body that often determines the details of a specific sanctions regime.
  - term: Chapter VII referral
    gloss: The Council, acting under Chapter VII, referring a situation to the ICC Prosecutor (Rome Statute art 13(b)), e.g. Darfur 2005.
