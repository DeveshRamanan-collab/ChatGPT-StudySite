plain: Rights are grouped into civil and political, economic, social and cultural, and collective "generations" — but all apply to everyone, have equal standing, and depend on one another.

formal: "First generation: civil and political rights (life, freedom from torture, expression, fair trial, political participation). Second: economic, social and cultural rights (education, health, adequate standard of living, work, cultural rights). Third: collective rights (self-determination; potentially development and a healthy environment). Universality: rights apply to all humans everywhere. Indivisibility and interdependence: all rights have equal status and cannot be ranked."
