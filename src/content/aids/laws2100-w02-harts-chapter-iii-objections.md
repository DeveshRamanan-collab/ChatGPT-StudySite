terms:
  - term: Duty-imposing rule
    gloss: A rule requiring or forbidding conduct, as much of the criminal law does; it can resemble an order backed by threats.
  - term: Power-conferring rule
    gloss: A rule giving people the power to do things legally, such as make a contract, a valid will or a marriage, by following a procedure. No duty to use it.
  - term: Tacit command
    gloss: "Austin's answer to custom: by not interfering, the sovereign tacitly accepts customary rules, so they are in a way its orders."
