  - name: Value propositions
    test: What value do we deliver? Which customer problems are we helping to solve, which needs are we satisfying? What bundles do we offer each segment? What is the minimum viable product?
  - name: Channels
    test: Through which channels do our segments want to be reached? How do other companies reach them now? Which work best, and which are most cost-efficient?
  - name: Customer relationships
    test: How do we get, keep and grow customers? Which relationships have we established, how are they integrated with the rest of the model, and how costly are they?
  - name: Revenue streams

  - name: Key resources
    test: What key resources do our value propositions require?
  - name: Key activities
    test: What key activities do our value propositions require?
  - name: Key partners
    test: Who are our key partners and suppliers? Which key resources are we acquiring from them? Which key activities do partners perform?
  - name: Cost structure
    test: What are the most important costs inherent to our business model? Which key resources and which key activities are most expensive?
