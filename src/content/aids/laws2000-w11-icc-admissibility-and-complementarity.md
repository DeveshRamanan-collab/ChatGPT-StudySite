plain: Even with jurisdiction, the ICC steps back if a state with jurisdiction is genuinely handling the case. It acts only where states are unwilling or unable, and only for cases grave enough.
concrete: "After the Prosecutor's investigation into the Philippines' \"war on drugs\" was authorised in 2021, the Philippines asked the Court to defer, saying national proceedings were under way. In January 2023 the Pre-Trial Chamber let the investigation resume, finding the national proceedings insufficient in scope — complementarity in action: the state's claim to be acting did not displace the Court once its proceedings did not genuinely cover the case."
formal: "The ICC is \"complementary to national criminal jurisdictions\" (art 1). Art 17(1): inadmissible where (a) a state with jurisdiction is investigating or prosecuting, or (b) investigated and decided not to prosecute — unless unwilling or unable genuinely to do so; (c) the person was already tried (art 20(3)); (d) insufficient gravity. Unwillingness: shielding, unjustified delay, lack of independence. Inability: collapse or unavailability of the judicial system."
