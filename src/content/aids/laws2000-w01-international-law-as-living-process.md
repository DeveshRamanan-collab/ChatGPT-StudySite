terms:
  - term: amendment
    gloss: A change to the provisions of an existing treaty — one way treaties evolve without being replaced.
  - term: protocol
    gloss: An instrument added to an existing treaty that forms a whole with it, used to extend or modify the treaty.
  - term: customary international law
    gloss: Law formed by general state practice plus a belief that the practice is law; the clearest example of law developing over time.
  - term: non-binding instrument
    gloss: A declaration or resolution, such as the Rio Declaration (1992), that guides states and builds momentum but creates no legal obligation.
