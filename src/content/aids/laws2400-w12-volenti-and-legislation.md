plain: "Statute pushes volenti both ways: it is shut off for passengers of drunk drivers, and widened for obvious risks of dangerous recreational activities, even risks the plaintiff did not know about."
formal: "Courts are generally reluctant to allow volenti against passengers who rely on an intoxicated driver, and some legislation excludes it (eg Motor Accidents Act 1988 (NSW) s 76). CLA s 5L expands the defence: a defendant is not liable for harm from the materialisation of an obvious risk of a dangerous recreational activity engaged in by the plaintiff, whether or not the plaintiff was aware of it (see Tapp)."
terms:
  - term: Dangerous recreational activity (s 5K)
    gloss: A recreational activity that involves a significant risk of physical harm.
  - term: Recreational activity (s 5K)
    gloss: Includes any sport, any pursuit for enjoyment, relaxation or leisure, and activity at places such as beaches and parks where people ordinarily do those things.
  - term: Obvious risk (s 5F)
    gloss: A risk that, in the circumstances, would have been obvious to a reasonable person in the plaintiff's position, even if it has a low probability of occurring.
