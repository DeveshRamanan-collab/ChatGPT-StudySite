plain: Individuals and groups hold human rights; states mainly owe them — both by not violating rights themselves and by protecting people from private abusers. International organisations can owe duties too.
concrete: The slides' example of a horizontal obligation is non-discrimination in housing. But the state's duty to ensure rights is fully discharged only if it protects people against private persons too; failing to do so is the state's own breach.
