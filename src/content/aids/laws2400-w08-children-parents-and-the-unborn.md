plain: Parents can owe others a duty to control their children, and owe their own children a duty not to create danger, but not a general duty about how they raise them.
concrete: "In Hahn v Conley, a grandfather was minding his 3-year-old granddaughter. She called out from across the road; he answered her call; she crossed and was struck by a car. The driver claimed an indemnity from the grandfather. The High Court held that the grandfather, in loco parentis, owed her no duty to protect her against that danger: he did not call her to come to him."
formal: "Parents may owe others a duty to control their child (Smith v Leurs: duty owed, not breached). Parents generally owe no duty to a child in feeding, clothing and raising, but must drive carefully and not create danger; they may be liable if the child is led into danger by their actions (Hahn v Conley). A child injured in the womb may sue for pre-natal injuries if later born alive."
terms:
  - term: In loco parentis
    gloss: Standing in the place of a parent, such as a grandfather minding a child; the carer is treated as a parent for the duty question.
  - term: Wrongful conception / wrongful birth
    gloss: "Parents' claims: the mother would not have conceived (conception), or the pregnancy would have been terminated (birth), but for the negligence."
  - term: Wrongful life
    gloss: A child's claim that the pregnancy should have been terminated because of handicap; controversial, e.g. how do you put a value on life?
misconceptions:
  - claim: Parents owe their child a duty of reasonable care in every aspect of upbringing.
    tempting: Parents are responsible for their children's welfare.
    correction: "Generally no duty in feeding, clothing and raising: parents may raise a child as they see fit. They must still drive carefully and not create danger for the child."
  - claim: In Smith v Leurs the parents were liable for their son's sling shot injury.
    tempting: The court held a duty was owed.
    correction: "A duty was owed but not breached: the parents had taken reasonable steps by telling their son not to use the sling shot outside their home."
  - claim: A wrongful conception claim is the child's claim that she should not have been born.
    tempting: The names of the three actions are similar.
    correction: That is wrongful life, brought by the child. Wrongful conception is a parents' claim that the mother would not have conceived but for the negligence.
