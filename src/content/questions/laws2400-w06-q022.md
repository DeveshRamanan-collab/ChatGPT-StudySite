stem: A farmer notices a bushfire smouldering on a neighbour's boundary but does nothing, and it later spreads to destroy the farmer's own crops instead. Applying Hargrave v Goldman's factors to the neighbour who did nothing, what is missing to found a duty if the neighbour did not even know of the fire?

    correct: true
