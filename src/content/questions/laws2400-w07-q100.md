  The issue is whether Vance owed Orchid a duty of care to avoid pure economic loss caused by negligent words. The loss
  is PEL. Reasonable foreseeability is likely — an investor might read and rely on a market report — but it is

  reliance or assumed responsibility toward it (Mallonland: an undertaking from which acceptance of care can be
  inferred). Conclusion: Vance very likely owed Orchid no duty.
