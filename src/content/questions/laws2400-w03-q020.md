model: Tom is likely liable for trespass to land. Trespass requires a direct and positive act of interference with land in the plaintiff’s possession, done intentionally or negligently, without consent or lawful excuse. Maria is in possession of the driveway (factual control and an intention to exclude others), so the possession element is satisfied. Tom’s act of walking onto the driveway and moving the bin was direct, positive and clearly intentional. There is no suggestion Maria consented to Tom entering to remove the bin, and being annoyed by a smell is not a lawful excuse. Because trespass to land is actionable per se, Maria need not prove any damage, although the mud left on the driveway would support compensatory (or aggravated) damages if pleaded. Maria should succeed in trespass to land against Tom.
