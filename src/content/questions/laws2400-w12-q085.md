  intoxicated to the extent that his capacity to exercise reasonable care and skill was impaired (s 50(1)). It is not

  determines to be appropriate. Conclusion: the club should argue for the s 50 presumption and a reduction of at least
  25%; it cannot use s 50(2) to defeat the claim, because the harm was likely anyway.
