stem: A rebel movement in State L commits abuses during a civil war and then succeeds in becoming L's new government. Under art 10(1), what follows?
options:
  - text: The abuses are attributed to L, because the movement became its new government.
    correct: true
    why: Correct — art 10(1) treats the conduct of an insurrectional movement which becomes the new government as an act of that state, even conduct before it was recognised as the government.
  - text: The abuses are never attributable, because at the time the rebels were fighting the government of L.
    why: Tempting because a good-faith state is not normally responsible for insurgents, but art 10(1) makes an exception once the movement becomes the new government.

    why: Tempting because art 10(2) does refer to a new state, but that applies where a movement establishes a new state in part of the territory; here it took over L's government.
