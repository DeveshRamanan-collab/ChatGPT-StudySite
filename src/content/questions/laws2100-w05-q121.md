Hart's Postscript, which reads as a reply to Dworkin, states that law can be identified by social sources — legislation, judicial decisions, social customs — without reference to morality, \"except where the law thus identified has itself incorporated moral criteria\" (CL 269). On balance inclusive positivism shows positivism can make room for morality, but not that it can explain all of Dworkin's principles."
