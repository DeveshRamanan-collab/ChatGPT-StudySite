stem: What does producing a "letter of full powers" demonstrate, under art 7 VCLT?
options:
  - text: That the person presenting it has been authorised by their state to negotiate and/or sign a particular treaty.
    correct: true
    why: Correct — full powers are exactly the document that confers the authority art 7 requires for anyone who is not one of the three automatically-exempt officials.
  - text: That the treaty has already entered into force for that state.
    why: Wrong — full powers relate to authority to negotiate or sign, an earlier stage than entry into force, which is governed separately by art 24.
  - text: That the person holds diplomatic immunity in the host state and may therefore sign any treaty the host state proposes.
    why: Tempting because diplomats carry special status abroad, but full powers are about authority to represent the state for a particular treaty, not immunity, and they do not licence signing whatever is proposed.
