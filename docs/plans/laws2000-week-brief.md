**Read docs/plans/2026-10-week-agent-rules.md first; where it differs from this brief, it wins.**

   `node scripts/extract.mjs raw/laws2000-pil/week-NN raw/laws2000-pil/unit-wide`. Week 11's 104.6 MB deck is read via
   its committed "(text extract).md". Skim content-src/laws2000-w07.yaml (first ~250 lines + a sample) as the finished
   model for this unit, and earlier weeks' concepts (src/content/concepts/laws2000-w0*.md) for depends-on/contrasts-with.
2. Weeks 8–12 have NO personal Weekly Notes. Build from the lecture slides (the spine: keep the lecturer's phrasing and
   order), the tutorial tasks and transcripts where present. Do NOT imitate the student's note-taking voice from
   weeks 1–7. Transcripts: w08 Part A (live) only — Parts B and C have none; w09 Parts A and B; w10–w12 none.
3. Problem unit (ILAC). MCQs test the rule/article that applies, its elements, the leading case, and common confusions.
   Short answers: turn EACH tutorial task question into a short answer, keeping the tutor's wording for the stem, with
   a model answer and rubric rows Issue, Law (authority), Application, Conclusion (+ top-band, margin-note). If the
   tutorial has more than 6 questions, keep all of them (≥ 6 total).
4. Treaty articles, UN Charter provisions and ICJ cases: only from the slides/tutorials/transcripts (confirmed in
   slides); check src/content/authorities (e.g. un-charter-*) and src/content/cases before adding.
5. Week 12 also has "Revision Slides" and "Tutorial Revision Quiz Questions": use them for Week 12's exam tips and
   short answers that belong to Week 12's topic (human rights law). A unit-wide revision set is built later by the
   lead — do not add questions to other weeks' concepts.
